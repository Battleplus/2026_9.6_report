# Strengths and Limitations

## Strengths

1. Heterogeneous graph better represents UAV-task relationships.
2. Graph reinforcement learning is suitable for dynamic allocation problems.
3. Event-triggered mechanism reduces unnecessary communication.
4. Attention improves important information aggregation.

## Limitations

1. Event trigger conditions may depend on manually designed thresholds.
2. Training requires large simulation environments and computational resources.
3. Sim-to-real transfer remains challenging.
4. Large-scale deployment may face communication and scalability issues.
5. Attention weights do not necessarily represent causal importance.
