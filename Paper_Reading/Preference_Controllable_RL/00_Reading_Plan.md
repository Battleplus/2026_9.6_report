# Preference Controllable Reinforcement Learning with Advanced Multi-Objective Optimization

## Paper Information

- Title: Preference Controllable Reinforcement Learning with Advanced Multi-Objective Optimization
- Venue: ICML 2025
- Topic: Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning (MORL), Preference Learning, Multi-Objective Optimization

## Reading Goal

Understand how preference-conditioned policies solve the trade-off controllability problem in MORL and analyze the connection with preference RL, RLHF and world model based decision making.

1. Motivation
   - Why conventional RL cannot handle conflicting objectives

2. Problem Definition
   - MOMDP
   - Pareto Front
   - Preference controllability

3. Method
   - Preference Controllable RL (PCRL)
   - Preference-conditioned policy
   - PreCo gradient manipulation algorithm

4. Theory
   - Pareto stationary convergence
   - Preference similarity convergence

5. Experiments
   - Fruit-Tree
   - MO-Reacher
   - HV and Cosine Similarity metrics

6. Research Inspiration
   - Preference RL
   - Multi-objective policy optimization
   - Potential combination with world models
